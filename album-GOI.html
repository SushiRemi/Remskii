Garden Of Impermanence

Garden Of Impermanence was supposed to be released a year before it actually came out, and was almost scrapped entirely. Making music, and existing in a world of so much pain and beauty is a cycle of birth and rebirth, one where I've had to rediscover and remake myself multiple times for my art to exist. Sometimes, you have to let go and just let things exist, even imperfectly.

Special thanks to Lauren for the art, Cassie for the support, and my beautiful Jaebird for the love.
