Letters From The Sixth Story

Letters From The Sixth Story is the ascent up a parking garage, to the sixth floor. I would frequent it whenever I was having an extremely depressive episode and peer over the edge, just wondering what would happen if I were to step off. Thankfully, it never came to that, but the songs I wrote detail that metaphorical ascent to the peak of instability, and the thoughts that might be running through my mind on the long way down.

Thank you to David for the cover art, and to Angel and Keira for everything else.
